Curated updates on PQC standards, regulatory timelines, and quantum technology milestones. Structured intelligence for decision-makers.

NIST
May 2026

NIST Finalizes First PQC Standards

ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), and SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) finalized for immediate adoption. NIST states these "can and should be put into use now."

Organizations should begin cryptographic inventory and migration planning immediately. The standards cover key establishment and digital signatures — the foundation of enterprise PKI, TLS, and code-signing infrastructure.

NSA
April 2026

NSA Reaffirms PQC as Primary Quantum Mitigation

CNSA 2.0 timelines established for national security systems. NSA does not recommend QKD for National Security Systems due to hardware and integration limitations.

Updated guidance reinforces PQC as the cost-effective, maintainable path. CNSA 2.0 mandates set clear deadlines through 2033 for all government and defense systems. Commercial equivalents expected across Five Eyes partners.

Industry
March 2026

Enterprise PQC Hiring Surges Across Security Industry

Booz Allen, Palo Alto Networks, and other major vendors post dedicated PQC roles as cryptographic inventory becomes a formal product category with dedicated budget.

Roles span cryptographic inventory, migration architecture, PKI/TLS readiness, vendor assessment, and executive risk reporting. This signals real budget allocation — not exploratory research — across regulated sectors.

NCSC
February 2026

NCSC Publishes Quantum Networking Classification Guide

Three-class framework helps enterprises evaluate when quantum networking is relevant vs. premature, covering QKD, networked sensors, and entanglement distribution.

The guide divides quantum networking into three classes: replacing classical security (QKD), extending networks with quantum components, and inherently quantum networks. Helps buyers avoid over-investing before technology matures for their use case.
