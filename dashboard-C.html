Glossary

Technical terms and acronyms used throughout this risk dashboard.

CNSA 2.0
Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite 2.0 — NSA-mandated suite of PQC algorithms for National Security Systems, with software deadline 2030 and hardware deadline 2033.
CRQC
Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer — a quantum computer capable of breaking current public-key cryptography (RSA, ECDSA). Estimated arrival 2030–2035.
FIPS 140-3
Federal Information Processing Standard 140-3 — security requirements for cryptographic modules, successor to FIPS 140-2. Transition deadline September 2026.
HNDL
Harvest Now, Decrypt Later — an active threat where encrypted data is collected today and stored for future decryption once CRQC becomes available.
ML-KEM
ML-DSA
ML-KEM (FIPS 203): Module-Lattice Key Encapsulation Mechanism. ML-DSA (FIPS 204): Module-Lattice Digital Signature Algorithm. NIST-standardized PQC algorithms.
QKD
Quantum Key Distribution — physics-based key exchange protocol using quantum states. Provides information-theoretic security but requires dedicated fiber or free-space optical links.
TDE
Transparent Data Encryption — database encryption at rest. Typically uses AES with RSA key wrapping. RSA wrapping is vulnerable to quantum decryption.
TRL
Technology Readiness Level — NASA/DoD scale from 1 (basic principles) to 9 (proven in operational environment). Quantum repeaters are currently TRL 3–4.
NISQ
Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum — current generation of quantum processors with 50–1000 physical qubits but insufficient error correction for fault-tolerant computation.
PKI
Public Key Infrastructure — the system of certificate authorities, registration authorities, and policies that manage digital certificates and public-key encryption.
