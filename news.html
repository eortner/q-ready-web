NIST
May 2026

NIST Finalizes First PQC Standards

ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), and SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) finalized for immediate adoption. NIST states these "can and should be put into use now." Organizations should begin cryptographic inventory and migration planning immediately. The standards cover key establishment and digital signatures — the foundation of enterprise PKI, TLS, and code-signing infrastructure.

NSA
April 2026

NSA Reaffirms PQC as Primary Quantum Mitigation

CNSA 2.0 timelines established for national security systems. NSA does not recommend QKD for National Security Systems due to hardware and integration limitations. Updated guidance reinforces PQC as the cost-effective, maintainable path. CNSA 2.0 mandates set clear deadlines through 2033 for all government and defense systems. Commercial equivalents expected across Five Eyes partners.

Industry
March 2026

Enterprise PQC Hiring Surges Across Security Industry

Booz Allen, Palo Alto Networks, and other major vendors post dedicated PQC roles as cryptographic inventory becomes a formal product category with dedicated budget. Roles span cryptographic inventory, migration architecture, PKI/TLS readiness, vendor assessment, and executive risk reporting. This signals real budget allocation — not exploratory research — across regulated sectors.

NCSC
February 2026

NCSC Publishes Quantum Networking Classification Guide

Three-class framework helps enterprises evaluate when quantum networking is relevant vs. premature, covering QKD, networked sensors, and entanglement distribution. Helps buyers avoid over-investing before technology matures for their use case.

Industry
May 2026

PQC Adoption Tops 50% Among Major Services

Google, Cloudflare, Apple, and Facebook have deployed PQC key exchange (X25519MLKEM768 and MLKEM1024). Microsoft, Amazon, Netflix, and GitHub have not. Certificate identity migration has not started for any major service — the "certificate identity gap" is universal. Cloudflare reports ~38% of global TLS traffic supports quantum-resistant encryption.

Standards
February 2026

China Approves First Quantum Computing National Standard

GB/T 47177.1-2026 establishes a unified framework for cloud-based quantum computing services. China is also developing independent PQC standards with a global call for algorithm proposals, focusing on "structureless lattice" approaches distinct from NIST. Organizations with China operations or supply chains should monitor this divergence.
